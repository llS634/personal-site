About project

MAX is a new comfort-class residential complex in Tyumen, the result of a collaboration between two leading developers—Точно and Творчество.

The MAX quarter is created for maximalists—for those who strive for more, who live brightly and want the most out of life. This became the basis of the slogan "Ignite life on MAX" and the spark metaphor: a small spark creates a firework of sensations.
