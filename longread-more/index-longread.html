Guide how to survive in the Middle Ages

Long read of an alien’s journey through the Middle Ages.

3D character

Longread

Project Idea

This project is inspired by the video How to Survive in the Middle Ages by Art For Introvert. My goal was to create an engaging long-read experience that combines modern design and 3D graphics with medieval imagery.

Wireframe

I began by analyzing the text from the video and organizing it into a clear content hierarchy. From this, I sketched a layout and developed the concept of a chessboard where the narrative would flow, integrating design with storytelling.

Concept

The project envisions an intergalactic tour set in medieval times, with an alien as the protagonist.

I created a 3D-rendered alien character to contrast with the medieval setting. This fusion of futuristic and historical elements adds a fresh and intriguing dynamic to the concept.

Identity Elements

The typography blends modern and medieval styles. For the main text, I used the standard Inter font to ensure readability.

The interface features neutral colors, emphasizing the visuals. The layout is divided into two sections: the right panel for text and the left panel resembling a chessboard to guide the story’s progression.

The alien character was created and rendered in Spline, with various poses and states.

Long-Read

Adapting the long-read for mobile was challenging, especially resizing images. I created modified versions for mobile and added a scrolling feature for larger images to maintain their quality and proportions.
